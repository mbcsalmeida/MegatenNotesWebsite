## General Information

## Stage 1-1

Turn off animations.

**1**: Tomoharu attacks top slime, Naoki kills it. Kaoru collects the chest.

**2**: Kaoru walks forward.

Tomoharu attacks slime, Naoki kills.

**3**: Kaoru and Tomoharu attack the Ustock, Naoki kills.

**4**: Kaoru attacks the Ustock below, Naoki kills it. Tomoharu heals Kaoru.

**5**: Kaoru grabs the chest with Legger Slum. Tomoharu goes to fountain, attacks the slime. Naoki equips, kills it.

**6**: Naoki/Tomoharu attack the last Ustock.

**7**: Occupy.

## Stage 1-2

**1**: Advance Kaoru.

**2**: Kaoru attacks the Zombie. Naoki kills slime. Tomoharu attacks Zombie.

**3**: Naoki kills Zombie. Tomoharu behind.

Kaoru goes to left chest.

**4**: Kaoru gets chest.

Naoki/Tomoharu advance.

**5**: Tomoharu attack Wendigo, Naoki kills.

Kaoru walks back to middle.

**6**: Everyone straight forward.

**7**: Kaoru kills Ustock. Naoki attacks the Wendigo. Tomoharu forward.

**8**: Kaoru straight forward, Naoki one step forward. Tomoharu advance to left, attack the Momunofu.

**9**: Tomoharu grabs Headgear. Kaoru attacks the Wendigo, Naoki equips and kills.

**10**: Tomoharu/Kaoru then Naoki kill the Momonofu.

Walk to Laboratorium.

## Stage 1-3

**1**: Kaoru attacks the Ustock. Naoki straight forward.

**2**: Naoki goes up, unequips items. Kaoru equips, attacks the Ustock.

**3**: Naoki fights Zombie. Kaoru opens door.

**4**: Save. Attack with Naoki, Kaoru advances.

**5**: Kaoru heals, Naoki attacks.

**6**: Kaoru attacks, Naoki walks to chest.

**7**: Kaoru attacks/walks to fountain, grabs chest.

Doesn't have to be precise after this. Kaoru kills the Tanki to get the Ring Boots, Naoki goes to the other side.

Once you get the Ring Boots equip everything on Naoki. Make sure to use the generator and use items freely.

## Stage 1-4

Kill all demons. Grab last two chests, make sure to use the fountain.
