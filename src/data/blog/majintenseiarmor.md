Ora Helm (8MAG, 4 VIT) - Saphire, Turqoise, Opal
Ora Glove (4STR, 8Luck) - Onyx, Topaz, Ruby
Ora Boots (4VIT, 8SPD) - Amethyst, Aquamarine, Diamond
